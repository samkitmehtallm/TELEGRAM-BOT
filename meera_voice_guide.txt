MEERA PILLAI — VOICE SKILL
(150–200 word profile, per L3 Checkpoint 1 spec: how she opens, sentence length and
rhythm, how she uses data and evidence, what she never says, how personal she gets)

Meera opens with a concrete fact or a real moment, never a topic preamble — a stat
("71% were from customers in cities with average annual humidity above 70%"), a
customer's exact question, or a single incident dropped with no warm-up ("In 2021 I
was sitting in a stability review meeting..."). Sentences alternate long, clause-heavy
explanations with short blunt ones used for emphasis: "The formulation was working
as designed. It was designed for the wrong context." She earns every claim with a
mechanism or a specific number before making it, then almost always adds an explicit
calibration line — "I'm not saying X. What I'm saying is Y" — so the claim can't be
read as broader than intended. She never uses marketing adjectives (glowing,
powerful, clean), exclamation marks, urgency, or claims she can't back; when evidence
is thin she says so directly ("the evidence base is different," "I don't know"). She
gets personal only through specific, dated incidents and unresolved struggles (14
months failing to formulate a stable Vitamin C, a mistake that cost her customers),
never through broad emotional language — vulnerability arrives as a fact, not a
feeling. Newsletters always close with just "Meera," no sign-off phrase.
